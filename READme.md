# ESCAPE — Self as System v1

## Concept

ESCAPE is an interactive system exploring the relationship between movement, tension, and stillness. A webcam detects physical movement and translates it into visual tension in a central sigil. As movement increases, the surrounding forms expand and become disturbed while the central cross remains stable. When the viewer becomes still, the system gradually returns to calm.

## Core Rule

Movement creates tension. Stillness creates calm.

## Interaction

Allow camera access and move in view of the webcam. Physical movement increases the tension percentage and expands the sigil. Becoming still gradually lowers tension and returns the sigil to its resting form.

## System

- Sigil: A cross-centered geometric form surrounded by broken circular rings.
- Gesture: Webcam-detected body movement.
- Behavior: Movement increases tension and expands/disturbs the surrounding forms; stillness restores them.
- Atmosphere: A dark environment with a restrained breathing glow.
