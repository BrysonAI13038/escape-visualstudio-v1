# v1 Decisions

## Keep Two
- The sigil remains recognizable while its surrounding forms transform.
- A slow breathing glow creates a calm, focused atmosphere.

## Cut One
- The separate clickable circles from the dissolve test. They felt disconnected from the sigil and required a second interaction system.

## Tighten One
- Movement will have defined limits. Small natural movements will create little change, while larger body movements will create stronger tension in the sigil.

## Ask One
- Can a camera-based system encourage the viewer to discover that physical stillness creates visual calm?
