SELF AS SYSTEM

System Name

ESCAPE

Rules

- Physical movement creates tension.
- More movement causes the sigil to expand and become more disturbed.
- Stillness gradually returns the sigil to its calm state.
- The central cross remains stable while the surrounding system changes.

Controls

Allow camera access. Move your body in view of the camera to increase tension. Become still to let the system return to calm.

V1 Reflection

In v2, I removed the tension meter so the sigil has to communicate tension on its own. I redesigned the sigil to connect more clearly to ESCAPE, with the cross as a stable center and the surrounding form representing pressure and distraction. Tightening the camera response made the connection between movement and tension easier to understand. Removing the meter made the system feel simpler and more focused. In v3, I want to take a risk by exploring how the system could react to different kinds of movement while keeping the same core idea.
