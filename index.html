SELF AS SYSTEM

System Name

ESCAPE

Rules

- Physical movement creates tension.
- More movement causes the sigil to expand and become more disturbed.
- Stillness gradually returns the sigil to its calm state.
- The central cross remains stable while the surrounding system changes.

Controls

Allow camera access. Move your body in view of the camera to increase tension. Become still to let the system return to calm.

V1 Reflection

In v1, the separate visual rule tests became one system where physical movement directly controls the sigil. Using the camera made the interaction feel more connected to the viewer because the system responds to body movement instead of a mouse or click. The part that feels most like me is the stable cross at the center while the forms around it react and change. The system can become visually busy at high tension, but the fixed center and limited visual language keep it from becoming random. The atmosphere stays dark and restrained so the movement remains the main focus. In v2, I would improve the motion detection so it can distinguish more clearly between small natural movement and intentional movement.
