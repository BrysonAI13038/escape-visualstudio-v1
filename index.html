ESCAPE

VISUAL RULE TESTS

01 — SIGIL SEED

Rule: The sigil is built from a small set of geometric forms that remain recognizable while rotating and subtly changing scale. I tested the movement and adjusted the rotation and pulse speeds so the transformation felt slow and controlled rather than distracting.

02 — BEHAVIOR RULE

Rule: Forms gradually dissolve when activated instead of disappearing immediately. A mouse click controls the activation, and I adjusted the dissolve speed so the transition felt controlled rather than abrupt.

03 — GESTURE LANGUAGE

Rule: The form responds to the user's proximity rather than a direct click. Mouse position controls the amount of separation and tension in the form, and I kept the response subtle enough that the original shape remains recognizable.

04 — ATMOSPHERE CONSTRAINT

Rule: Light is concentrated around the active form while the surrounding space remains dark. The Canvas uses a slow breathing glow as its only atmospheric change, and I kept the movement subtle so it changes the mood without creating visual noise.
